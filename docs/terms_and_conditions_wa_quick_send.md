## 2. Description of the Service

WA Quick Send is a lightweight utility that lets you open a WhatsApp chat with a phone number **without saving it to your contacts**. Features include:

- **Instant Click-to-Chat:** Enter a number in various formats; the App cleans it, applies your default country code, and opens WhatsApp with an optional pre-filled message.
- **OCR Scanning:** Extract phone numbers from your camera or an uploaded image.
- **Local History:** View recent numbers and assign custom names, stored only on your device.
- **Settings and Data Control:** Set a default country code, export history as CSV, and clear all data.
- **Installable App:** Add to your home screen and use with limited or no connectivity.

**The App does not send messages itself.** It only prepares a link that opens WhatsApp on your device. Sending the message happens inside WhatsApp, under WhatsApp's terms.
