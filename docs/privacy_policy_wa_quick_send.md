# Privacy Policy

**WA Quick Send**

**Effective Date:** October 2026
**Last Updated:** October 2026

---

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how **WA Quick Send** ("**App**", "**we**", "**us**", "**our**") handles information when you use our web application and installable Progressive Web App (PWA).

WA Quick Send is built on a **privacy-first, local-first** principle. The App lets you open a WhatsApp chat with a phone number you have not saved, using manual entry or by scanning a number with your camera or an image. **We do not operate a backend that collects, receives, or stores your personal data.**

By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

---

## 2. Summary (Plain-Language)

- Phone numbers, messages, scanned images, and history **stay on your device**.
- Camera scanning and text recognition (OCR) run **locally** on your device.
- We do **not** have user accounts, and we do **not** sell or share your data.
- You can **export** or **permanently delete** your history at any time.
- When you tap "Open in WhatsApp", the number and message are handed over to **WhatsApp**, which is governed by WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.

---

## 3. Information We Handle

### 3.1 Information Processed Locally on Your Device

The following is processed and stored **only on your device** and is never transmitted to our servers:

| Data | Purpose | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| Phone numbers you type, paste, or scan | To open a WhatsApp chat | Your device (browser/app storage) |
| Pre-filled message text | To pre-fill the WhatsApp message | Your device (temporary, in-memory unless saved in history) |
| Recent numbers (history) | Quick re-engagement with recent numbers | Your device's local storage |
| Custom names/aliases you assign | To help you identify numbers | Your device's local storage |
| Default country code and preferences | To auto-format numbers | Your device's local storage |
| Images or camera frames used for scanning | To extract phone numbers via OCR | Processed in device memory; **not saved or uploaded** by the App |

### 3.2 Information We Do Not Collect

We do **not** collect or require:

- Your name, email address, or phone number
- Account credentials (there are no accounts)
- Your contacts or address book
- Your WhatsApp messages, chats, or media
- Your location
- Advertising identifiers

### 3.3 Technical Information from Hosting

The App is delivered over the internet through a hosting provider (GitHub Pages). Like most websites, the host may automatically log basic technical information when files are requested, such as IP address, browser type, device type, and request timestamps. This is used for security, reliability, and abuse prevention and is governed by the hosting provider's privacy policy. We do not use this information to identify you individually.

## 4. Device Permissions

| Permission | Why we ask | Can I refuse? |
|---|---|---|
| **Camera** | To scan phone numbers from business cards, flyers, or screens | Yes. You can still type numbers or upload an image instead. |
| **Photos / File access** | To let you select a screenshot or image for scanning | Yes. You can still type numbers manually. |

Camera frames and selected images are used only to detect phone numbers on your device. You can revoke permissions at any time in your browser or device settings.

## 5. How We Use Information

Because data stays on your device, the App uses it only to:

1. Format phone numbers (including applying your default country code).
2. Generate a WhatsApp link and open the chat with an optional pre-filled message.
3. Show your recent numbers and aliases for convenience.
4. Let you export your history as a CSV file.

We do **not** use your information for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making.

## 6. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not have access to your local data, so we cannot sell, rent, or share it.

### 6.2 Handoff to WhatsApp

When you tap **"Open in WhatsApp"**, the App launches WhatsApp (via a `wa.me` / WhatsApp deep link) with the phone number and any message you entered. From that moment, your use of WhatsApp is governed by **WhatsApp's Terms of Service and Privacy Policy**. We are not responsible for WhatsApp's practices.

### 6.3 Hosting Provider

Our hosting provider may process technical logs as described in Section 3.3.

### 6.4 Legal Requirements

Since we do not hold your personal data, we generally have nothing to disclose. If required by law, we will cooperate with valid legal requests to the extent of any information we actually hold (for example, hosting logs, if available to us).

## 7. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

- **Storage:** History and settings are stored in your browser's/PWA's local storage on your device.
- **Retention:** Data remains until you clear it, clear your browser/site data, or uninstall the App.
- **Security:** Local processing reduces exposure risks. However, anyone with access to your unlocked device or browser profile may be able to view your history. We recommend using your device's screen lock and keeping your software up to date.
- **No cloud backup by us:** We do not back up your data. If you clear site data or lose your device, we cannot recover it. Use the CSV export if you want a backup.

---

## 8. Your Choices and Controls

- **Clear History:** Use the **"Clear History"** option in Settings to permanently erase all locally saved data.
- **Export:** Use **Export** in Settings to download your history as a CSV file.
- **Edit/Remove Aliases:** Edit or remove custom names directly in the History drawer.
- **Revoke Permissions:** Disable camera or file access in your browser or device settings.
- **Uninstall:** Remove the App from your home screen and clear site data in your browser to delete everything stored locally.

**Important about exported CSV files:** Once exported, the file is on your device and under your control. You are responsible for protecting it, especially if you upload it to a CRM or share it with others.
