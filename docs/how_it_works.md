# What is WA Quick Send?

WA Quick Send is a simple utility for sending WhatsApp messages to unsaved phone numbers. It bypasses the need to add temporary contacts (like delivery agents, stores, or leads) to your phonebook just to send a single message.

It is **not** a messaging app or a WhatsApp client. You still send your actual messages through your normal WhatsApp app.

---

# Why does this exist?

Saving every temporary phone number to your address book creates clutter. Worse, it exposes your personal profile picture and status to strangers, because WhatsApp relies on your contact book for privacy settings. 

WA Quick Send solves this by generating a deep link that forces WhatsApp to open a chat with a specific number without needing it to be saved.

> **Important:** This tool processes everything locally on your device. We do not store, see, or transmit the numbers you type or scan.

---

# Step-by-step workflow

### 1. Enter a Number
Type or paste the phone number. The app will automatically format it and prepend your Default Country Code (which you can configure in Settings).

### 2. Pre-fill a Message (Optional)
If you want to send a standard greeting, type it into the message box. For example: "Hi, here is the document we discussed."

### 3. Open WhatsApp
Tap the **Open in WhatsApp** button. This will seamlessly switch you over to your WhatsApp app with the chat open and your message pre-typed in the text box.

### 4. Scan from Camera (Alternative)
Instead of typing, tap the **Scan Number** button. Point your camera at a business card or upload a screenshot. The app will automatically read the text, find the phone number, and fill it into the box for you.

---

# Pro Tips
* **Save Names:** In the History drawer, tap the pencil icon next to any number to give it a custom name (e.g., "Plumber").
* **Install the App:** Tap the browser menu and select "Add to Home Screen" to install WA Quick Send like a native app. It will even work offline!
